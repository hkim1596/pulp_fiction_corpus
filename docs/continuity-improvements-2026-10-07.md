# Automated story continuity — 7–8 October 2026

Rules version 2.6 and the updated publication pipeline prioritize retaining a complete story through column changes, advertisements, page furniture, and continuation notices. These decisions run automatically; diagnostic flags do not block ordinary publication. Existing human corrections remain authoritative and are not overwritten. The historical migration additionally preserves previous output when it cannot prove where existing identity metadata or cross-issue links belong.

## Algorithm changes

- OCR results retain the source scan number. Missing, duplicate, incomplete, or changed page inputs cannot be accepted as a completed issue. Partial readings retry in a fresh attempt directory; complete output has a source manifest.
- Story assembly resumes after advertisement pages even when only one body paragraph follows. Generic resumption requires positive evidence before treating an opening as a competing department or advertisement; short dialogue is not rejected merely because it lacks conventional prose signals. The check uses column position so an advertisement in an adjacent column cannot block the story. Explicit forward references take precedence, including a short ending above another story's title. Wrapped and abbreviated continuation notices are recognized, and a notice naming a different piece cannot redirect the current story.
- On a mixed page, a later continuation heading cannot turn all preceding columns into advertisements. An adjacent unfinished sentence, lowercase run-on, multiple narrative paragraphs, and a separate commercial column establish a conservative continuation. Explicit offers cannot provide the narrative anchors, ordinary prices cannot prove advertising, and incomplete column selection is declined. A single intervening catalogue page requires repeated printed product and offer evidence and no narrative column or short dialogue. Once the complete continuation check succeeds, its previously misassigned boxes are preserved in a separate advertisement record, reconnecting the narrative directly across the page.
- Duplicate printed page numbers are resolved using neighbouring folios. An unsupported duplicate cannot silently become a continuation destination. A letters or house section after an explicit story ending owns its own continuation notices.
- A missing display title can be recovered from an exact byline, printed installment description, nearby contents entry, and subsequent running title. A department's exact heading and running title can override a corrupt contents-page number. An author-only contents line can bind to its unique printed title/byline instead of creating a false start inside another story.
- A contents-page title can identify an ordinary-size heading within a page. This prevents the next piece from taking the preceding story's ending.
- Contents entries with an inline byline are separated into title and author. Matching ranks all candidate pairs so the actual title wins over a cover credit sharing its author. Title-only matching needs meaningful word evidence; similar letter shapes are insufficient. Combined title/byline boxes and serial labels between a title and byline are recognized. Explicit poetry sections retain their first verse as reading text.
- False bylines in prefaces, next-issue announcements, signed testimonials, and fictional documentary statements no longer create new stories without supporting identity evidence. These checks include positive controls for genuine printed story starts.
- Long body paragraphs mislabelled as page headers, or lacking a drop cap, are protected from automatic furniture/teaser removal.
- Reading order uses column geometry, spanning headings, and separate horizontal bands while preserving all page/region identities. Numbered picture panels, narrow or slightly overlapping gutters, and short sidebars have explicit regression coverage. Ambiguous geometry retains the OCR sequence.
- Model splits require an independently printed title or byline. Chapter headings and continuous sentences remain attached. A tail split cannot take a later resumption of the original story. Removing known story text as advertising, furniture, a caption, or a notice requires supporting printed evidence. Rejected proposals remain in the audit metadata.
- Outputs replace complete files atomically. Failed website database builds remain pending and retry automatically. A failed identity or coverage check in the optional experimental assembly preserves that entire variant byte-for-byte while independently validated rules and publication can proceed.
- Website edits, publication, and migration share an issue-level filesystem lock. Edit forms carry the source assembly's SHA-256 digest, so a form opened before a rebuild cannot attach an annotation to reused article IDs. The migration blocks annotation submissions while installing snapshots and keeps its marker in place if recovery remains unresolved.
- CPU worker processes use spawn rather than inheriting live OCR subprocess pipes. Timeout cleanup is bounded; completed OCR jobs cannot hang indefinitely because an unrelated worker holds an output pipe open. Two subsequent live 148-page jobs completed, validated, assembled, and advanced automatically after this repair.

## Development benchmark

Frozen human annotations cover 42 verified stories and 349,689 words labelled as reading text. Candidates are evaluated without replaying those corrections onto them. Cached model decisions are reused; no additional model inference was needed for comparison.

| Measure | Previous publication pipeline | Updated publication pipeline |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annotated body words retained in one story | 335,794 | 349,494 |
| Body-word recall | 96.026% | 99.944% |
| Stories with false splits | 5 | 0 |
| Extra reading words | 1,275 | 556 |
| Gold body words assigned to non-story records | 405 | 0 |

The 13,700-word recovery is a development-set result, not a corpus-wide accuracy estimate. The remaining 195 apparent missing words are documented disagreements about epigraphs, recap text, an end marker, and a subtitle. A raw order metric reports 29 inversions in a recovered *Demolished Man* passage: the frozen annotations contain a moved paragraph whose position conflicts with printed folios and narrative continuity. The labels were not silently changed to improve the score. The rules-only comparison introduces no verified continuity regressions and recovers a 165-word annotated ending.

Original scans were also checked for the small-heading boundary in *Mustang Mesa* and the prize sidebar in *Baffler Contest*. The former confirms that two different pieces occupy the same page; the latter confirms that the sidebar must not interrupt surrounding paragraphs.

Production backfill failures provided additional source-based regression cases. The corrected assembly retains all 497 regions of *The Mad Verdict*, separates *The Chronicler* from *The Atomic Pile* and retains its 17,590-word published text, and identifies the 16,484-word *Brood of the Dark Moon* and 8,849-word *The Heads of Apex* as their own stories. *The Merry Wives of Murder* retains 13,142 words across a facing-page title and advertising columns, including continuation text previously assigned to advertisements. The July 1948 installment of *Dreadful Sanctuary* spans scan pages 76–151: rules assembly contains 1,008 regions and 32,564 words, while guarded publication removes one 41-word book-order coupon, leaving 1,007 regions and 32,523 words. Misread contents entries had assigned much of it to other sections. These counts describe the resulting OCR text; they are not independent accuracy estimates.

The final mixed-column follow-up restores 67 region keys across three source-confirmed run-on boundaries in two Cosmopolitan issues; one is a printed notice, and no prior narrative text is removed. Six previously misassigned catalogue boxes are also preserved in a separate advertisement record so they no longer interrupt a sentence. The main failures at scan 136 of November 1932 and scan 194 of October 1930 account for 50 narrative boxes. Some affected records already have incorrect titles or earlier merged ownership; this repair reconnects their continuous body text and does not claim to correct those identities.

This benchmark was used during development. Independent held-out annotations across more magazines and layouts are still needed to estimate generalization. Fully automatic execution does not establish 100% reconstruction accuracy or restore text absent from a scan/OCR result.
